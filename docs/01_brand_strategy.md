## 1. The 30-Second Brand Read

DASH is a hyperlocal, celebration-based customer acquisition platform for small experiential businesses (yoga, pilates, spa, ice cream, escape rooms, paint & sip, boutique fitness). It connects people within 5 miles of a merchant who are celebrating a birthday or anniversary with offers from that merchant. Built on Bubble.io. Currently runs a 90-day free trial, $11.99/mo afterward, no contract.

**The DASH wedge:** Groupon trained merchants to expect discount-junkie customers who come once, demand 70% off, and never return. DASH inverts that — celebrators are neighbors, repeat-prone, and emotionally pre-disposed to spend because it's *their* day.

## 2. What DASH Actually Sells (Mechanism)

DASH is **not** a coupon platform. DASH is a **neighborhood celebration network**.

| Layer | What it is | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Surface offer** | "Post an offer, get new customers on their birthday" | Easy for a merchant to grasp in 10 seconds |
| **Real product** | Geofenced, intent-matched introductions to repeat-capable customers | Solves the #1 small-business problem: predictable foot traffic |
| **Unique mechanism** | The Celebration Trigger — proximity (5mi) + life event (birthday/anniversary) + spending intent, fused into one signal | This is the proprietary edge that makes DASH ≠ Groupon ≠ Yelp ≠ Birthday Club |
| **Hidden product** | A monthly recurring relationship at the price of a pizza, that returns 10–50x ROI on a single repeat customer | This is what gets the close |

> **The Mechanism Name:** *"Celebration Proximity Marketing"* — DASH owns this category.

## 3. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

### Primary (Tier 1 — fastest close, highest LTV)
- **Boutique fitness studios** — yoga, pilates, barre, EMS, hot yoga
- **Massage / day spa / med-spa**
- **Escape rooms**
- **Paint & sip / experiential learning**
- **Personal training studios**
- **Ice cream / dessert / experience-driven food**

### What unites them
1. Owner is operator (decision-maker answers the phone)
2. Margin > 60% on a celebration package
3. Customer experience is "the thing being sold" (not a SKU)
4. Currently spending $200–$2,000/mo on Groupon, Yelp, or Meta ads with declining ROI
5. Average transaction is $25–$200 — perfect celebration price point

### Avoid for now
- Chain locations with no local decision authority (e.g. Crunch, StretchLab without owner contact)
- Pure retail with no experiential element
- Restaurants doing >$1M (DASH economics are too small)

## 4. The Real Pain DASH Solves

Most small experiential businesses live and die on **3 problems**:

1. **Groupon's death spiral.** Discount junkies arrive, demand the deal, never come back, leave 3-star reviews, and Groupon takes 30–50%.
2. **Yelp/Google ads cost $4–$12/click with no intent filter** — a click ≠ a paying body in the studio.
3. **They have no system to capture the most profitable customer they will ever serve: someone celebrating something on the day they're already in the mood to spend.**

> "You don't need 1,000 more visitors. You need 30 birthday celebrators a month who walk in, light up, and tell their friends. That's a $50K business shift."

This is the line.

## 5. Competitive Landscape

| Player | Their Promise | Their Real Problem | DASH Advantage |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Groupon** | "Cheap deals near you" | Trains customers to expect 70% off, takes ~30–50% of fee, one-and-done buyers | DASH customers are local + repeat-prone, no rev share, flat fee |
| **Yelp Ads** | "Be visible to searchers" | High cost per click, no purchase intent guarantee | DASH only delivers people in a *celebration* mindset within 5mi |
| **The Birthday CLUB** | Same problem space — birthday-triggered acquisition | Closer competitor; need to differentiate on radius + anniversary trigger + price | DASH = anniversary too, hyperlocal 5mi, $11.99 entry vs higher monthly |
| **Meta / Google ads** | "Reach everyone" | Massive cost, requires creative + targeting expertise SMBs don't have | DASH is one-click setup, zero ad budget needed |
| **Word-of-mouth / referrals** | Organic | Slow, unpredictable, can't be scheduled | DASH is referral-on-rails: a celebration is the most shared moment in a person's month |

**Defensible moat:** Two-sided network. Each celebrator who has a great experience tells 4–6 people. Each merchant who gets 1–2 great celebrators tells other merchants. Compounding referral mechanics on both sides.

## 7. Emotional Buying Drivers (Kennedy / Sugarman)

DASH merchants don't buy "marketing." They buy:

1. **Relief** — "Finally something that's not Groupon"
2. **Pride** — "I'm the studio that throws the best birthdays in the neighborhood"
3. **Belonging** — "I'm part of the local community network, not a stranger to my own customers"
4. **Control** — "I post the offer I want, when I want, no rev share"
5. **Hope** — "This might actually save my Tuesday afternoons"
6. **Fear of missing out** — "If three studios on my block are on DASH and I'm not, I lose the celebrators to them"

Every page, every script, every email — frame against these six drivers, never against features.

13. **AI cross-merchant referral engine** — "Studio X loved DASH. They're 0.6mi away. Should we introduce you?"
14. **AI cohort analytics report** — auto-generated monthly insight pack to merchants
