## Typography
- **Display / Headlines:** Fraunces (variable serif) — weights 600, 700, 800
- **Body:** Inter — weights 300, 400, 500, 600, 700
- Both available from Google Fonts

## Logo
- Wordmark: "DASH" in Fraunces 800
- Lockup: Black rounded square `D` mark + wordmark
- Always 8px rounded corners on the mark

## Photography Rules
- Real merchants only. No stock.
- Real candles, real moments.
- Warm light. Natural skin tones.
- Phone-shot is fine — authenticity > polish.

## Spellings — Non-Negotiable
- DASH — all caps
- Loren AI — two words, both capitalized
- "Powered by LOREN AI" — in footer of every DASH page
