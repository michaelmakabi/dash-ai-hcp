Real answers

The questions every owner asks. Answered honestly.

How is DASH different from Groupon?
Groupon's model trains customers to expect 70% off — and takes 30–50% of what's left. DASH is a flat $11.99/mo. We don't take a cut of your sales. And our customers are neighbors celebrating something, not discount-hunters passing through.
How do I know how many people came through DASH?
Two ways: a live dashboard you can log into anytime, and a simple text we send every Friday with the number. Most of our merchants tell us the text is way easier.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. No contract. Cancel any time. We only want you on the platform if it's working for you.
How do you get celebrators to my store?
We run paid placement across Meta, Google, and SMS push to people inside your 5-mile radius who are celebrating that week. Plus a word-of-mouth flywheel — every celebrator can invite 3 friends to your offer.
How much does it cost?
First 90 days are completely free. After that it's $11.99 a month. No setup fee, no rev share, no surprises. Less than a large pizza.
Does it actually work?
Only you can decide that for your studio — which is why the first 90 days are free. Most merchants need just one celebrating party of 4–6 people walking in to recover an entire year of fees.
How long does setup take?
About 2 minutes. We'll walk you through it on the phone, or you can do it from your phone in the studio while we're on the line.
What kind of offer should I post?
Whatever you'd give a friend's friend on their birthday. Could be a free class, a complimentary upgrade, or a small package. Most merchants use the offer that makes the celebration *feel special* — not the offer that's the steepest discount. We'll help you write it.
